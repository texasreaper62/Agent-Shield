# Privacy Policy for Agent Shield

**Last updated:** March 17, 2026

## Overview

Agent Shield is a Chrome browser extension that scans web pages for AI-specific threats such as prompt injection, hidden manipulation, and AI-powered scams. Your privacy is our top priority.

## Data Collection

**Agent Shield does not collect, store, transmit, or share any user data.** Period.

- No personal information is collected
- No browsing history is tracked
- No website content is sent to external servers
- No analytics or tracking tools are used
- No cookies are set by the extension
- No user accounts or sign-ups are required

## How It Works

All threat detection runs **entirely on your device** within your browser. When you visit a web page, Agent Shield scans the page content locally using pattern-matching logic bundled inside the extension. Scan results are stored temporarily in your browser's local storage for display purposes only and are never transmitted anywhere.

## Permissions

Agent Shield requests the following browser permissions:

- **activeTab**: To display scan results for the page you are currently viewing when you click the extension icon.
- **Host permissions (all URLs)**: To scan web pages for AI threats as you browse. No page content ever leaves your device.
- **Storage**: To save your extension preferences (such as enabled/disabled state) locally on your device.

## Third Parties

Agent Shield does not share data with any third parties because Agent Shield does not collect any data.
